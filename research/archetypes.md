# Synthetic recruits · industry archetypes

Owner: Research and Insights · Created 2026-11-25 for sprint 2

**These people are fictional.** Each is a composite of a common role in licensed cannabis receiving and inventory work, written by the team because the founder couldn't recruit real leads this sprint. Nothing they "say" in a synthetic session is user evidence. It's the team's best guess, written down so it can be challenged. Real sessions with 3 to 5 leads stay open as research debt.

**Evidence grade for anything derived from these: Synthetic (weak).** It can surface likely confusion, edge cases and questions for real sessions. It can't validate a design or meet a success measure on its own.

| | Archetype | Role and setting | How they work | What they care about | What would make them distrust Mellow |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A1 | **Dana, receiving lead** | A high-volume retail store with several deliveries a day | Mostly on a phone at the back door, often with a box in one hand. Interrupted constantly. | Getting a delivery accepted correctly and quickly, and not missing a mismatch that bites later. | Anything that pops up mid-count, or makes her tap twice to get rid of it. |
| A2 | **Marcus, inventory manager** | Responsible for inventory across a few locations | Desktop most of the day, with spreadsheets alongside. Reviews what leads left for him. | Knowing what changed since he last looked, and handoffs he can act on without re-asking. | Notes that don't say *when* or *which check*, or that repeat things he's already seen. |
| A3 | **Priya, compliance coordinator** | Prepares for inspections, owns the audit trail | Careful reader. Screenshots things for records. | That nothing the tool says can be read as a compliance claim, and that every action leaves a trail. | Wording like "OK", "verified" or "checked" that an inspector could read as an attestation. Dismissing things without a record. |
| A4 | **Luis, distribution warehouse clerk** | Bulk inbound and outbound manifests at a distributor | Works through long lists fast, mostly by keyboard. Newer to the job. | Not slowing down. Clear words, not jargon. | Labels he has to guess at. Stamps or badges that look like approvals. |
| A5 | **Jo, owner-operator** | A single small store; does receiving, inventory and compliance herself | Bursty: catches up at the end of the day on a laptop. | Seeing the few things that matter, fast, then leaving. | Noise. Three notes where one would do. Anything that makes her feel behind. |

**Coverage check.** Phone-first (A1), multi-location and handoffs (A2), compliance and audit (A3), keyboard and high volume with a new hire (A4), and low-time generalist (A5). Missing: a manager who only reviews handoffs, and anyone using assistive technology. Both need real recruits.
