# Sprint 2 sessions · Mellow speaking first

Owner: Research and Insights · Facilitator: the founder (or a delegate) · 3 to 5 receiving leads · 30 minutes each, remote or in person

## What we need to learn

1. When Mellow speaks first with a one-line note, do leads understand what it means and why it appeared?
2. Does a note help them act sooner, or does it feel like an interruption?
3. Do they notice and understand the "Compared" stamp on answers? (This is the open question from sprint 1.)

## Who to recruit

People who receive and reconcile cannabis inventory, or review manifests, on shift: receiving leads, inventory managers, or compliance coordinators. Aim for a mix: at least one person who works mostly on a phone, and at least one manager who gets handoffs.

**Recruiting message (edit freely):**
> I'm testing an early tool that helps receiving teams spot mismatches between inventory and receiving records. Could you give me 30 minutes to try a prototype and tell me what's confusing? It uses sample data only; nothing from your business is needed.

## Before each session

- Use the prototype link Research sends (sample data only).
- Ask permission to take notes. Don't collect names or business details beyond their role.
- Say plainly: "We're testing the tool, not you. There are no wrong answers."

## Script

**Warm-up (3 min)**
- "Walk me through what happens when a delivery arrives on your shift."
- "When records don't match, how do you find out today?"

**Task 1 · First look (5 min).** Open Today.
- "What's going on here? What would you do first?"
- Listen for whether they notice the MELLOW note, and what they think it is.

**Task 2 · A note appears (7 min).** The facilitator triggers a new check that adds a difference.
- Don't point at the note. Wait 20 seconds.
- "Did anything change?" Then: "What is that telling you? Why do you think it showed up now?"
- "If you were mid-task, would this help or get in your way?"
- "How would you make it go away?"

**Task 3 · Asking (7 min).**
- "Ask Mellow whether PKG-443 is OK to sell." Then: "What did it tell you? Would you trust that?"
- Point to the answer: "What does that 'Compared' label mean to you?" Then let them open it.
- "Ask it how it knows."

**Task 4 · Too much (5 min).** The facilitator triggers three notes at once.
- "How does this feel? What would you want to happen instead?"

**Wrap-up (3 min)**
- "What's one thing that would make you trust this more? One thing that would make you turn it off?"
