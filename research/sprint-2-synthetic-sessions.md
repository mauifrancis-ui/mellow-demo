## Predicted findings

1. **On a phone, the row note won't be seen.** The queue is a collapsed sheet, so A1 relies on the sheet bar's "1 new" and the briefing. The proposal already covers this. Keep it, and make sure "1 new" sits in the pinned bar.
2. **"Since 8:43" is ambiguous** (A2). People read it as the last time *they* looked. Say "Since the 8:43 check".
3. **Dismissing needs a trail** (A3). Compliance staff expect anything that hides information to be recorded. Log notes shown and dismissed in Activity as Mellow events, never as decisions.
4. **"Compared" can read as an approval to new staff** (A4). It's the same risk Compliance raised in sprint 1, and it's now predicted in use. Keep the open-to-explain disclosure, and put this question first in real sessions.
5. **Batching matters most to the busiest and the smallest teams** (A5, A1). Three notes for one check would read as noise.
