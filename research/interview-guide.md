# Market validation kit · V1 (sprint 7)

**Goal:** primary evidence for two things.
1. **The need:** retail receiving teams in tracked states lose real time or risk when their receiving log, manifests and inventory disagree.
2. **The edge** (`research/positioning.md`): what they lack isn't detection, which their POS already does. It's the evidence behind each correction, kept together, plus a comparison at the dock across all three records.

Don't mention the positioning hypothesis to the person. Ask what they do; don't ask whether they'd want it.

**Who:** five people, each at a different licensed retailer in a tracked state (for example Colorado, Michigan, Missouri):
- receiving leads;
- inventory or compliance managers;
- one owner-operator.

**Format:** 30 minutes by video, with screen-share of the demo at the end. **The founder sends all outreach. Mellow never contacts anyone.**

## Outreach draft (for the founder to edit and send)

> Subject: 30 minutes on receiving and inventory records?
>
> Hi [name], I'm Maui. I'm researching how retail teams handle it when the receiving log, the manifest and inventory don't match. I'm not selling anything; there's a prototype at the end if you're curious. Would you have 30 minutes in the next two weeks? Happy to work around your shift.

## Questions (listen more than ask)

Ask 1 to 9 before showing the demo, so the demo doesn't shape the answers.

**The problem**
1. Walk me through the last delivery you received. What did you check, and where did you write it down?
2. When the manifest, the receiving count and inventory don't agree, what happens next? Who decides?
3. How often does that happen in a typical week? What was the last one?
4. What did your last fix cost in time, start to finish? Did it need a correction in the state system, a call to the supplier, or a manager?

**The edge (tests `research/positioning.md`)**
5. How many systems does one package's record live in, from the manifest to the shelf? Where does the receiving count go first: paper, a spreadsheet, the POS?
6. What do you use today to catch a mismatch (POS report, the state system, spreadsheets, memory)? What do you like about it, and what does it miss?
7. After you correct a package in the state system, what else do you keep? Where is it, and could you find it a year from now?
8. When an inspector or auditor asks about a discrepancy, how do you show them you investigated it? What did they ask to see last time?
9. Do your stores all run the same systems? Any plans to change?

**After the demo**
10. What would you trust in what you just saw, and what wouldn't you?
11. Who would need to approve a tool like this, and what would it need to replace?

## What to capture (for `research/market-analysis.md`)

- Mismatches per week, time per mismatch, and who resolves them.
- Current tools and what they miss.
- Words they use. Content and Voice will check Mellow's labels against them.
- Signals of willingness to pay, and who pays. Don't ask about price directly.
- Anything that contradicts the hypothesis. Record it first.
- **The positioning kill criteria** (`research/positioning.md`). For each person, mark yes, no or unclear:
  1. Is the state-system note alone enough for inspectors, with no other records kept?
  2. Does their POS already keep the evidence with each correction, and are they happy with it?
  3. Is the receiving count already in the POS at the dock (two records, not three)?
  4. Are discrepancies rare (under about one a week per store) or cheap to fix?
  5. Do they run one system end to end, with no plan to change?
