What it does

What Mellow does, and what it doesn’t.

Does

  • Compares lot numbers between your inventory export and your receiving log, one package at a time.
  • Puts the packages where the records disagree first, then the ones missing a detail.
  • Shows what it compared and when, on every answer.
  • Speaks first only when a check finds something new.
  • Keeps the trail of decisions and notes, and exports it as CSV or print.
  • Links the Texas hemp rules with their status and dates, as references.
  • Remembers your columns, and clears everything from your device in two steps.

Doesn’t

  • Decide for you. A person records the issue, marks it not an issue, or leaves it for a manager.
  • Judge your records against the law. It links Texas hemp rules but doesn’t check your records against them.
  • Score your readiness, predict an inspection, or predict what lawmakers will do.
  • Read photos or attachments yet.
  • Send your files anywhere. In the demo, they stay in your browser.

How a shift goes

Four steps, the same every time.

  1. 01Compare two exportsYour inventory export and your receiving log, as CSV files.
  2. 02Start with what disagreesThe briefing names the first package and says why it goes first.
  3. 03Check the label, then decideRecord the issue, mark it not an issue, or leave it for a manager. Your evidence goes with it.
  4. 04Hand off the shiftActivity keeps what was decided and what’s still waiting, ready for the next person.

Built to be checked

Every answer shows its work.

Ask Mellow about a package and the answer ends with what it compared: which files, which package, and when. Tap it to see them. If Mellow can’t trace something to your records, it says so.

  • The stampOn every answer drawn from your records. Answers that aren’t say “Not from your records”.
  • Why this?On every note Mellow raises first: the trigger, and what it looked at.
  • ActivityThe day’s decisions and notes, including the ones that were dismissed.

On the floor

On a phone, Today opens on your next package. The bar at the bottom always says how many need you.

Try it on your files

Two CSV files, two columns.

Comparisons run in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

FilesAn inventory export and a receiving log.
ColumnsA package ID and a lot column in each file. The templates use package_id and lot_number; other common names like “Package Label” or “Batch No” work too.
RowsOne row per package. Up to 500 KB per file.
PrivacyRemove names and anything you wouldn’t share. Mellow only needs the two columns.
